IN PLAIN ENGLISH

  • cons. — 'consensus' — the average analyst forecast. Markets move on the RESULT vs this expectation, not the result alone.
  • FOMC — the US Federal Reserve committee that sets interest rates — its decisions move nearly every market.
  • risk-off — money rotating OUT of risky assets (stocks, crypto) into safe havens (cash, gold, dollar). 'Transition' means it's only just starting.
  • VIX — the market's 'fear gauge'. Higher = traders expect bigger swings; ~20 is the long-run average.
  • hawkish — leaning toward higher rates / tighter policy — generally a headwind for stocks.
  • yield — the interest rate on a bond; rising yields make bonds more attractive than stocks and raise borrowing costs.
  • capex — capital expenditure — money a company spends building for the future (data centres, chips); good if it earns a return, a drag if it doesn't.
  • SPY — the S&P 500 — the 500 largest US companies; the market's main benchmark.
