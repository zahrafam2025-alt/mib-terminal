Privacy

No cookies. No tracking. Only what you choose to share.

MIB Terminal and its arcade use no cookies, no advertising, no analytics service and no tracking pixels. Nobody signs up: there is no account, no e-mail and no password.

What is kept, and where

WhatWhereWhy, and for how long
The nickname and avatar you pickIn your browser (and shown next to your messages and scores)So friends know who is who. Until you change or clear it below.
Your scoresThe scoreboard serverThe top 10 per game, under your nickname. Only the best scores stay.
Your chat messagesThe chat serverSo others can read them. Each room keeps only its last 200 messages.
A random browser numberIn your browser, and with your messagesSo a reload brings you back to your place in a game, and so you can delete your own messages. It is not linked to who you are.
Visit countsThe site's serverHow many visits each day, and the most people online at once. Counts only: no address, no device, no history of pages.
Your light or dark choice, your game settingsIn your browser onlySo the site looks the way you set it.

Please pick a nickname rather than your real name. The pages are served by GitHub Pages and the scores and chat by Cloudflare: like any host, they see your internet address while they answer you. Our own server keeps it for one minute in memory only, to stop score spam, and never writes it down.

Delete what is yours

This removes the messages you wrote from this browser, then clears your nickname, avatar and settings from it. Scores stay on the boards under the nickname (they carry no other information).

Translations and links

The “Français” button opens the page through Google's page translator; Google then sees the page you translate. Headlines link to the news sites that published them.
